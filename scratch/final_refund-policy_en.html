MST Import and Export Sdn. Bhd. (“MST”) supplies frozen seafood, frozen food, meat, ingredients and other temperature-sensitive products.

Because these products are perishable and temperature-sensitive, refund and return requests are subject to the following conditions.

1. 12-Hour Notification Requirement

For a product-quality complaint to be considered, the customer must notify MST within 12 hours of receiving the goods.

The notification should include the order or invoice number and relevant photographs or videos where applicable.

2. Product Must Remain Frozen

For a refund or replacement request to be considered, the affected product must:

  • Remain frozen
  • Not have been thawed
  • Not have been cooked
  • Not have been processed, repacked or altered
  • Remain available for inspection where reasonably required

This requirement allows MST to properly assess whether the issue originated from the product.

3. Valid Quality Issues

A refund or replacement may be considered only where MST verifies a genuine product-related quality issue or an issue attributable to MST or the supplied product.

Examples may include:

  • Genuine product quality defect
  • Wrong product supplied
  • Material difference from the confirmed order
  • Damage attributable to MST during delivery
  • Other verified product-related defects

4. Personal Cooking Method and Preference

Refunds will not be accepted solely because of:

  • Personal cooking method
  • Personal preparation method
  • Personal taste
  • Personal texture preference
  • Personal expectation
  • Dissatisfaction with the result after cooking
  • The customer's preferred way of preparing the product

For example, if the product is cooked differently from the recommended or customary preparation method and the customer does not like the resulting taste or texture, this will not automatically constitute a product quality issue.

5. Change of Mind

Refunds or returns will generally not be accepted because:

  • The customer changed their mind
  • The customer ordered the wrong item
  • The customer no longer requires the product
  • The customer does not like the product
  • The customer prefers another brand or product
  • The customer does not like the taste or texture

This does not affect rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded.

6. Evidence and Investigation

MST may request:

  • Order or invoice number
  • Photographs or videos
  • Product label and batch information
  • Delivery records
  • Description of the problem
  • Storage information
  • Handling information
  • Other reasonable information required to investigate the complaint

MST may inspect the affected product before approving a refund or replacement.

7. Disposal of Product

Customers should not dispose of an affected product before contacting MST, unless disposal is reasonably necessary for health or safety reasons.

Where the product has already been thawed, cooked, disposed of or otherwise altered, MST may be unable to verify the alleged product-quality issue and the refund request may not be accepted, subject to applicable law.

8. Approved Refunds

If MST confirms that a refund is appropriate, the refund will be processed using the applicable payment method or another arrangement agreed with the customer.

Processing time may depend on the relevant payment provider or bank.

9. Replacement / Credit Note / Other Resolution

Depending on the circumstances, MST may provide:

  • Replacement product
  • Refund
  • Partial refund
  • Credit note
  • Other reasonable resolution

The appropriate resolution will depend on the nature of the confirmed issue.

10. Custom-Sourced Products

Custom-sourced or specially ordered products may not be eligible for cancellation, return or refund simply because the customer changes their mind or no longer requires the product.

Specific terms will be communicated before confirmation where applicable.

11. Wholesale and B2B Orders

Wholesale and B2B orders may be subject to additional commercial terms stated in quotations, invoices, purchase orders, sales confirmations or separate agreements.

Those specific terms will apply to the relevant transaction to the extent permitted by law.

12. Customer Responsibility

Customers are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate order information
  • Providing accurate delivery information
  • Receiving products promptly
  • Properly storing frozen products
  • Maintaining suitable storage conditions after delivery
  • Following reasonable product-handling instructions

To the extent permitted by applicable law, MST is not liable for deterioration caused by improper customer storage, handling, thawing, cooking, delayed acceptance, or circumstances outside MST's reasonable control.
