1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They may help websites operate properly, remember preferences and understand website usage.

2. How We May Use Cookies

MST may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Operate essential website functions
  • Remember preferences
  • Support language or display settings
  • Improve website performance
  • Understand website traffic
  • Improve user experience
  • Support website security
  • Analyse website usage
  • Support third-party website functions where enabled

Optional cookies or similar technologies will be used according to the visitor's cookie preferences and the services enabled on the website.

3. Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: These may be required for basic website functions, session handling, security and other functions necessary for the website to operate. Examples include session functionality, security, cart functionality, and remembering cookie-consent preferences.
  • Preference Cookies: These may remember visitor preferences such as language (EN / ZH / BM), currency (MYR / SGD / USD) or display settings.
  • Analytics Cookies: Where enabled, analytics cookies may help MST understand how visitors use and interact with the website.
  • Third-Party Cookies: Third-party services integrated into the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies, such as analytics, maps, social media, security or communication services, where enabled.

4. Managing Cookies

Visitors may manage their cookie preferences through the Cookie Settings available on this website and may also manage or disable cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.
