MST Import and Export Sdn. Bhd. (“MST”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information provided to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how MST may collect, use, disclose, store and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, submit an enquiry or RFQ, place an order, request customised sourcing, arrange delivery, or otherwise interact with our business.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Refund & Return Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy and Cookie Policy.

Depending on how you interact with MST, we may collect:

  • Name

  • Telephone or WhatsApp number
  • Email address
  • Billing and delivery information
  • Product enquiries and RFQs
  • Sourcing requirements
  • Order and transaction information
  • Payment and transaction information required to process or confirm transactions. Payment card details may be processed directly by third-party payment providers.

  • Website usage and technical information
  • Information collected through cookies and similar technologies
  • Other information reasonably necessary for us to provide our products and services

2. How We Use Personal Information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and RFQs
  • Prepare quotations
  • Process and fulfil orders
  • Arrange delivery or collection
  • Provide customised sourcing services
  • Process payments
  • Provide customer service
  • Manage B2B and wholesale accounts
  • Maintain business and transaction records
  • Verify information where reasonably necessary
  • Improve our website, products and services
  • Prevent fraud, misuse or unauthorised activity
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations
  • Send relevant business or promotional communications where permitted by law

We may disclose relevant information where reasonably necessary to operate our business or provide services.
